Match Score Formula (Transparent Matching USP)

Our platform breaks down the match score mathematically based on skills (70% weight) and interests (30% weight):

Match Score = [ (Common Skills / Mentee's Skills) × 70 ] + [ (Common Interests / Mentee's Interests) × 30 ]
  • Skills Weight (70%): Evaluates technical competencies (e.g., Java, Spring Boot, MySQL).
  • Interests Weight (30%): Aligns long-term career domains (e.g., Backend, Cloud, AI).
  • Tie-Breaker: Mentors with equal match score are ranked by years of experience.
